Fiverr Seller Fees 2026: What You Keep After the 20% Cut

TrustScore Editorial Team · Updated November 21, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer: for a standard completed Fiverr order, a seller receives 80% of the purchase amount. Fiverr's commission is the remaining 20%, including on Gig Extras and tips. That is your starting net — not necessarily the amount that reaches your bank after optional services, withdrawal costs and applicable taxes.

This is the seller-side companion to our Fiverr fee calculator, which focuses on the buyer's checkout total. The two sides of the transaction are different: buyers pay a service fee at checkout, while the seller's earnings share is calculated separately.

How Fiverr's 20% seller commission works

Fiverr's official Payment Terms say that each successfully completed standard order credits the seller with earnings equal to 80% of the purchase amount. Fiverr's Help Center clarifies that the 80% applies to the order, including Gig Extras and tips. In practical terms, the platform's standard seller commission is 20%.

This does not include income tax, currency conversion, withdrawal charges, advertising or subscriptions. If you offer multiple packages, calculate each tier separately and include the time required for revisions and communication.

Are buyer fees deducted from the seller's 80%?

No. Fiverr's buyer service fee is a separate checkout charge. Fiverr's Payment Terms state that, as of September 2025, the buyer service fee for Gigs is 5.5% of the purchase amount, with an additional $3.50 on purchases under US$200. The buyer sees the checkout total before paying. The seller's standard earnings calculation remains 80% of the purchase amount.

That distinction matters when you quote a price. Do not subtract the buyer's checkout fee from your own 80% calculation, and do not promise a buyer that your Gig price is their final checkout total. For the buyer-side calculation, use our Fiverr buyer fee calculator.

Withdrawal fees and when money becomes available

The 80% earnings figure is not the same as immediately withdrawable cash. Fiverr applies a safety clearance period after an order is marked complete. Its Payment Terms describe 14 days for standard sellers and 7 days for Top Rated sellers. Your account's eligibility and any current account restrictions take precedence over a general guide.

Fiverr's Help Center lists withdrawal costs by method. The published schedule includes PayPal at $0, bank transfer through Payoneer at $1, and a Payoneer account withdrawal at $3. Availability, minimums, transfer times and provider charges can vary by country and can change, so check the withdrawal screen in your own account before setting your prices.

Other costs sellers should separate from the commission

  • Fiverr Ads: optional advertising spend. Treat it as a marketing cost, not as part of the standard commission.
  • Seller Plus or other subscriptions: optional plans can add recurring costs. Confirm current price and eligibility in your account before subscribing.
  • Taxes: Fiverr notes that sellers are responsible for applicable taxes in their jurisdiction. Indirect taxes may also be displayed or withheld where required by law.
  • Currency conversion: your payment provider or bank may apply its own exchange-rate spread or conversion fee.
  • Refunds and cancellations: cancelled or reversed earnings are not ordinary completed-order income. Review the account activity and platform rules for the specific order.

A practical way to calculate your real net

  1. Start with the order price, including any extras and tips.
  2. Multiply by 0.80 to estimate standard seller earnings.
  3. Subtract any optional advertising or subscription cost you attribute to that order.
  4. Subtract the withdrawal charge and any provider or currency-conversion costs.
  5. Set aside the amount required for taxes under your local rules.

Keep a simple monthly record of gross order value, Fiverr earnings, withdrawals, optional costs and tax reserves. This gives a more useful picture than looking only at the Gig price or the balance shown before a withdrawal.

What to verify before publishing or changing your prices

  • Check Fiverr's current Payment Terms for the current seller share and buyer fees.
  • Check the Fiverr Earnings page guide for how completed orders, extras, tips and available balances are displayed.
  • Check withdrawal methods and fees for your location.
  • Use the buyer fee calculator when you need the client's checkout total.

Frequently asked questions

What percentage does Fiverr take from sellers?

For standard completed orders, sellers receive 80% of the purchase amount, including Gig Extras and tips. Fiverr's seller commission is the remaining 20%.

If I charge $100 on Fiverr, how much do I keep?

The standard seller earnings are $80 before withdrawal costs, optional services and taxes.

Does the buyer service fee come out of my 80%?

No. The buyer service fee is added separately to the buyer's checkout total. It is not deducted from the seller's 80% earnings share.

How long does Fiverr hold seller earnings?

Fiverr's terms describe a standard 14-day safety clearance period after order completion and a 7-day period for Top Rated sellers. Check your account for eligibility and current status.

How much should I charge to earn $100?

Divide $100 by 0.80. A $125 order produces $100 in standard seller earnings before withdrawal costs and taxes.
