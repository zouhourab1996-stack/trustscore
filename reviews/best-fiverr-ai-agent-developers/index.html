TrustScore: 8.3/10. Fiverr can be useful for prototyping a narrowly defined AI agent, but the deliverable should include permissions, tests, logs and a safe handover—not only a polished demo.

Short answer: start with one low-risk workflow, require human approval for external actions and never share unrestricted production credentials.

What separates a serious listing

Fiverr reports a sharp rise in AI-agent searches. Our buyer gap is the operational detail often missing from marketplace pages: what the agent can access, how failures are handled and how the owner can stop or audit it.

Our minimum order checklist

  • Trigger, tools, data boundaries and success metric.
  • Normal tests plus refusal and failure cases.
  • Logs, rate limits, cost limits and an emergency stop.
  • Source code, documentation and ownership terms.
  • A sandbox or test branch before production deployment.
