What is worth buying

Short-form repurposing, caption cleanup, silence removal and a one-video pilot are easy to inspect. A faceless-channel package can work when you already know your niche and approve the script, voice and footage sources separately. The service becomes risky when the seller promises an audience outcome instead of a file.

The rights question buyers forget

Ask who owns or licenses every component: AI voice, stock clips, music, images, fonts and generated graphics. Also ask whether the delivered file includes captions and whether the project file is included. A video that looks finished but cannot be legally published or edited is not a bargain.

Our recommended first order

  1. Send one source video and a short audience brief.
  2. Order one 30–60 second vertical clip.
  3. Request two hooks and one caption style, not unlimited creative direction.
  4. Review pacing, factual accuracy, spelling, audio and rights.
  5. Only then discuss a weekly package.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Large choice of fixed-scope editors
  • Fast pilots and clear package comparisons
  • Useful for repetitive captioning and repurposing

Cons

  • AI quality and rights vary by seller
  • Cheap packages often exclude source files
  • Results like views cannot be guaranteed

Verdict

Buy a pilot, not a promise. Fiverr earns the 7.4 for choice, speed and protected checkout, but the buyer has to define the deliverable. If you need a strategist who owns the channel outcome, hire for that separately.
