What a data analyst actually does for a small business

A data analyst takes numbers you already have and turns them into answers. That might mean finding out which products make money, why sign-ups dropped in March, or which ad channel brings customers who stay. The work usually has four parts: cleaning the data, analysing it, showing it in charts, and explaining what it means.

Many buyers hire the wrong kind of expert. A data analyst answers questions from existing data. A data engineer builds the pipes that move data around. A data scientist builds predictive models. If you only need a monthly sales report, an analyst is enough and far cheaper.

What really moves the price

Two projects can both be called "analyse my sales data" and differ by ten times in price. These are the factors that matter, in the order they usually bite.

  1. Data mess. Fiverr's guide says cleaning can take 40 to 60 percent of total project time on messy datasets. Duplicates, missing values and mixed formats cost real hours.
  2. Volume. A single CSV of 5,000 clean rows might take 3 to 5 hours. Several databases with millions of records can take 40 to 60 hours.
  3. Method. Describing the past is cheapest. Predicting the future roughly doubles the time. Recommending actions through optimisation costs the most.
  4. Deliverable. A PDF report adds about 2 to 3 hours. An interactive dashboard with drill-downs can add 10 to 20 more.
  5. Tools. Excel and Google Sheets sit at base price. Tableau, Power BI, Python or R add about 15 to 30 percent, according to Fiverr's guide.
  6. Industry. Healthcare, finance and pharma analysts can charge 20 to 40 percent more because the rules are strict.

The good news is that you control the first two. A cleaned file and a precise question are the cheapest ways to cut a quote.

What you pay Fiverr on top

The gig price is not the final price. Fiverr adds a buyer service fee at checkout. Fiverr's help pages and independent cost guides describe it as 5.5 percent, plus a flat $3.50 on orders under $200. On a $100 gig that is $9.00, so the order costs $109.00. On a $250 gig it is $13.75, so you pay $263.75.

The 9-point vetting checklist

Most bad Fiverr data jobs fail for the same reasons: a vague brief, a seller who never saw the real data, and numbers nobody checked. Use this checklist before you order.

  1. Read reviews for data work, not generic praise. Look for reviews that mention Excel, SQL, a dashboard or a report, with details.
  2. Open the portfolio. You want sample dashboards or reports with clear charts and short explanations, not only screenshots.
  3. Ask one test question. "How would you handle missing values in a sales column?" A good analyst gives a specific, reasoned answer.
  4. Send a small paid sample first. Use a basic package on 500 to 1,000 rows. It costs little and shows how they work.
  5. Agree the tool. Name the file format and platform (Excel, Sheets, Power BI, Tableau, Looker Studio, Python) before you pay.
  6. Agree the definitions. Define words like "customer", "active" and "revenue" in writing. Different definitions create different numbers.
  7. Ask for the method. You should receive the cleaned file, the formulas or queries, and a short note on assumptions.
  8. Check three numbers yourself. Recalculate a total, an average and a count from the raw data. If all three match, trust the rest more.
  9. Keep it on your account. Build the dashboard in your own workspace and invite the seller, so you keep it if the seller disappears.
Rule of thumb: never accept a dashboard you cannot explain in two sentences. If you cannot say what a chart shows and where its data comes from, ask for a short walkthrough video.

How to write a brief that gets accurate quotes

A good brief is the single biggest saving. Use this template and paste it into your first message.

  • Goal: one sentence, such as "Find which three products drive most of our profit in 2026."
  • Data: file type, number of rows, and where it came from (shop export, ad platform, spreadsheet).
  • Known problems: duplicates, blank cells, mixed date formats, anything you already suspect.
  • Output: for example, "5 charts in Google Sheets plus a one-page summary."
  • Tool: where it must live and who needs access.
  • Deadline and revisions: the date you need it and how many change rounds you expect.
  • Privacy: which columns you removed and what the seller may not share.

Privacy first: Strip names, emails, phone numbers and payment details before sharing a file. If the data is regulated, such as health or financial records, ask for an NDA or look at Fiverr Pro or an agency.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Buying the cheapest gig. A $5 gig that returns charts built on wrong filters costs more in bad decisions than a $100 job done properly.
  • Skipping the sample. One small paid test saves arguments later.
  • Handing over live databases. Share exports, not admin logins.
  • Accepting numbers without a method. Ask how each metric was calculated.
  • Forgetting follow-up questions. Fiverr's guide says new questions often add 20 to 40 percent to the original project cost, so keep a small reserve.

FAQ

How much does it cost to hire a data analyst on Fiverr?

Fiverr's 2026 cost guide for business data analysts says basic analysis and reporting starts around $21, mid-tier work such as data cleaning and standard dashboards runs about $58 to $110, and advanced projects like predictive models or financial analytics run $120 to $300 or more. Its scope tiers put basic projects at $41 to $125, intermediate at $90 to $200 and advanced at $160 to $300+. These are marketplace figures, so a clear brief with real data is what moves you toward the middle of the range.

Is Fiverr or Upwork better for hiring a data analyst?

Fiverr suits fixed-price, well-defined jobs such as cleaning one file, building one dashboard or running one analysis. Upwork suits open-ended or hourly work. Upwork Insights, an independent index, reports a median advertised rate of $30 an hour for data analysts with $10K+ in public earnings, with the middle half asking $19 to $50. Neither platform guarantees quality, so vetting matters on both.

What should I send a data analyst before they start?

Send a sample of the real data with sensitive fields removed, the business question in one sentence, the tool you want the result in (Excel, Google Sheets, Power BI, Tableau, Looker Studio or Python), and a list of the three charts or numbers you need. Add the deadline and how many revision rounds you expect. A seller can quote accurately only when they have seen the messy parts of your data.

Is it safe to share company data with a Fiverr freelancer?

Share the minimum. Remove names, emails, phone numbers and payment details before you send a file. Use a sample for the first order, keep the dashboard on your own account (Google, Microsoft or Tableau) and invite the seller as a collaborator. For regulated data such as health or financial records, ask for a signed NDA or use a vetted Pro seller or an agency instead.

How long does a Fiverr data analysis project take?

Fiverr's guide says basic projects usually finish in 3 to 5 business days, intermediate multi-dataset work takes 1 to 2 weeks, and advanced predictive modelling takes 2 to 4 weeks or longer. Dirty data is the usual cause of delay, because cleaning can take 40 to 60 percent of the total time on messy files.
