What you actually get

The sales letter wraps a liver-support formula (milk thistle territory, plus a 'Mediterranean ritual' narrative) in weight-loss promises. Liver health genuinely matters for metabolism — that part isn't fantasy — but the leap from 'supports liver function' to 'dissolves fat overnight' (their words) is where marketing takes over from biology. Fulfillment is reliable, the funnel is mature, and the 60-day ClickBank window applies. Best treated as a liver-support supplement with a weight-loss story attached, not the reverse.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

People attracted to the detox/liver angle who want a stable, high-volume operation with a real refund channel.

✘ Skip it if…

Anyone who hears 'overnight fat dissolve' and believes it, or who wants clinical evidence for the weight claims specifically.

Pros & cons

What we liked

  • The liver angle is at least adjacent to real physiology, unlike most niche mythology

  • Formula label is published on the official site
