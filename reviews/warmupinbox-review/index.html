What you actually get

Connect a sending inbox (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or custom SMTP — setup is genuinely about thirty seconds), set a daily volume, and the platform's network of real inboxes starts exchanging realistic mail with it: opened, replied-to, marked important, pulled out of junk where it landed. Over two to four weeks this ramps a cold mailbox into a sender spam filters tolerate. Monitoring rounds it out — placement checks and reputation signals so you see the climb instead of guessing. The honest caveat: warmup is increasingly bundled into sending tools (Woodpecker ships it built-in), so this is the tool for senders whose stack doesn't include it — or for warming more mailboxes than a sender's bundled allowance covers.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Cold-email teams and agencies with new domains or mailboxes to prepare — especially anyone whose sending tool doesn't include warmup, or who warms many inboxes at once.

✘ Skip it if…

Anyone not sending cold email (regular newsletter platforms manage their own reputations), and users of tools that already bundle warmup — check before double-paying.

Frequently asked questions

What is WarmupInbox?

An email warmup platform: its network of 30,000+ real inboxes exchanges realistic mail with your sending mailbox — opens, replies, spam-rescue — to build the sender reputation cold email depends on.

Do I need it if my outreach tool has warmup built in?

Maybe not — Woodpecker and several modern senders include warmup natively. Check your tool first; WarmupInbox earns its subscription when your stack lacks it or you need more warmed inboxes than the bundled allowance.

How much does it cost?

From $15 per inbox per month with tier discounts, and a 7-day free trial. Check the site for current pricing.

How long does warmup take?

Plan on 2–4 weeks before a new mailbox is ready for real campaign volume — reputation is earned gradually, and any tool promising overnight trust is selling you impatience.
