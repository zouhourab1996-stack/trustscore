What you actually get

Instantly is a cold-email platform: unlimited sending mailboxes with built-in warmup, a sequence builder, a shared reply inbox (Unibox) and, as separate lines on the bill, a B2B lead database and AI agents. The entry price is low, which is why it appears in nearly every cold-email roundup, but the bill is modular because sending and lead credits are sold as separate subscriptions. Most reviews quote the $47 Growth plan and stop there. The pricing page we read on November 3, 2026 lists sending plans from $47 to $358 a month, credit plans from $47 up, and bundles at $94, $194 and $555.

What the Terms of Service say

The Terms (last updated November 2, 2026) name the operator as Foo Monk, LLC. Five clauses matter before you enter a card:

  • No refunds (§5.3). "All fees paid under this agreement are non-refundable and non-cancellable," whether the service is used, unused or terminated early. Section 5.1(b) adds that unused fees are not refunded even pro rata.
  • Auto-renewal at the current price (§5.1(c), §5.2). Paid plans renew automatically, and Instantly may raise fees at the start of any renewal term.
  • 21-day lock-out (§5.1(b)). You cannot re-subscribe to the same paid monthly plan within 21 days on the same account.
  • Credits (§5.7). They expire at the end of the subscription period. If the plan renews, they can roll over twice, then they are lost.
  • Arbitration (§12). Disputes go to binding arbitration, with a class-action and jury-trial waiver.

What reviewers say in public

On November 3, 2026, Instantly's Trustpilot page showed no star rating. It carried a notice that the rating is unavailable because of a breach of Trustpilot's guidelines, and said a number of fake reviews had been removed. The page lists 1,182 reviews, 326 of them in the last 12 months. Frequent topics are customer service, cancellation, refunds and subscriptions.

The newest reviews we read, posted between late October and November 2, describe billing and a forced redesign. One three-year customer says an add-on was cancelled over a late payment and not reinstated after the invoice was paid. Another reviewer reports warmup problems after the update. A five-year user, by contrast, still calls it the easiest tool for launching campaigns. These are individual opinions, not findings.

  • Quality & depth 7.8: unlimited mailboxes and warmup, sequences, AI agents and a large claimed lead database in one product.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Agencies and outbound teams sending tens of thousands of cold emails a month who want unlimited mailboxes at a flat fee, and who are comfortable managing domains, DNS and deliverability themselves.

✘ Skip it if…

Anyone who needs a refund safety net or one predictable bill, and first-time senders who have not set up sending domains yet. Woodpecker is the deliverability-first alternative in our reviews.

Pros & cons

What we liked

  • Unlimited email accounts and unlimited warmup on every Outreach plan, including the $47 one (pricing page)
  • A $47 entry point covers 5,000 emails and 1,000 uploaded contacts a month, low for the category
  • Sending scales to 500,000+ emails a month, with a dedicated-IP system (SISR) on Light Speed and Agency
  • AI Sales Agent and a vendor-claimed 450M+ contact database are bundled from the $94 Starter bundle
  • A five-year customer on Trustpilot still calls it the easiest tool for launching campaigns

What could be better

  • No refunds: Terms §5.3 makes every fee non-refundable and non-cancellable, including unused or early-ended months
  • Modular pricing: sending, credits and add-ons are separate lines, so a working setup costs $94 to $555 a month, not $47
  • The pricing page disagrees with itself on Hypergrowth (125,000 vs 100,000 emails a month), and earlier 2026 reviews quote an older $97 price
  • Trustpilot shows no star rating for instantly.ai (guidelines breach, fake reviews removed); recent reviewers describe billing, cancellation and a forced redesign
  • Credits expire with the billing period and roll over at most twice (Terms §5.7)
  • Disputes go to binding arbitration with a class-action waiver (Terms §12)

Frequently asked questions

How much does Instantly cost in 2026?

On November 3, 2026 the pricing page listed sending plans at $47 (Growth), $107 (Hypergrowth) and $358 (Light Speed) a month, lead and AI credit plans from $47, and bundles at $94 (Starter), $194 (Scale) and $555 (Agency). Annual billing lowers the sending plans, for example Growth to $37.60 a month.

Does Instantly offer refunds?

No. Section 5.3 of the Terms of Service says all fees are non-refundable and non-cancellable, whether the service is used, unused or ended early. Monthly plans cannot be re-subscribed within 21 days on the same account (section 5.1(b)). A free trial exists, so test before you pay.

Why do Instantly reviews quote different prices?

Sending, credits and bundles are priced separately, and some reviews quote the monthly rate while others quote annual billing. Several earlier 2026 reviews list Hypergrowth at $97 a month; the pricing page showed $107 on November 3. Confirm the figure at checkout.

Is Instantly good for deliverability?

It includes unlimited warmup and monitoring features, but no tool can guarantee inbox placement. Some reviewers report that warmup scores did not match real placement, so verify your lists and test placement before scaling.
