Best Fiverr Mobile App Developers: Buyer Review

TrustScore: 8.0/10. Fiverr can be useful for a small, well-defined app or MVP, but “iOS and Android app” can mean anything from a clickable demo to a production release. The buyer must define the platform, backend, testing and store handover before ordering.

Short answer: start with a written MVP and one milestone, keep your Apple and Google developer accounts in your name, and require source code plus a test build.

What the marketplace gap looks like

Fiverr has a large mobile-app category, but generic listings often hide the most important scope questions: native or cross-platform code, backend ownership, device testing, store submission and recurring services. A low starting price is not a complete app budget.

Questions to ask before ordering

  • Is the app native, Flutter, React Native or another stack?
  • Does the price include backend, authentication, payments and push notifications?
  • How many screens, user roles and revision rounds are included?
  • Which iOS and Android versions and physical devices will be tested?
  • Who owns the repository, API keys, design files and third-party licenses?
  • Does store submission include only the upload or also rejection fixes?

Hidden costs

Budget separately for Apple and Google developer accounts, hosting, databases, maps, email or SMS services, analytics, paid APIs, design assets and post-launch maintenance. Never let a developer create the only owner account for a critical service.

Red flags

  • A full “Uber-like” or “social network” app promised for a tiny fixed price.
  • No test build before final delivery.
  • Requests for your personal passwords instead of collaborator access.
  • Unclear source-code handover or refusal to work in your repository.
  • Store approval promised as a guarantee.

Verdict

Recommended for a controlled MVP or defined feature. TrustScore 8.0/10 reflects the range of capable developers, balanced against the risk of vague scope and recurring infrastructure costs. Start small, keep ownership in your accounts and release only after independent testing.

FAQ

How much does a mobile app cost on Fiverr?

It depends on screens, platforms, backend, integrations, testing and store support. Compare the full scope rather than the starting package.

Should I build iOS and Android at the same time?

For a first MVP, one platform or a carefully chosen cross-platform stack can reduce risk. Decide based on your audience and maintenance plan.

Who should own the app store accounts?

You or your company should own the Apple and Google developer accounts. Give the developer the minimum collaborator access required.
