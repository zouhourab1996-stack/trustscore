Liv Pure — ClickBank Listing

A liver-first 'purification and fat-burning complex' that was once one of ClickBank's highest-gravity offers. Demand has collapsed and buyer reviews are poor, so we no longer recommend it.

Not recommended Listing, not a review — see the note below.

⚠️ Verified facts (checked October 8, 2026)

  • Demand has collapsed. Liv Pure's ClickBank gravity (a rough measure of how many affiliates are making sales) is about 27, down from a peak of about 799, with a conversion rate around 0.15% (CBSnooper).
  • Trustpilot: 2 out of 5 from 16 reviews, almost all 1-star. Complaints include refunds that didn't arrive, double charges from add-ons, shipping deducted from refunds, slow email-only support and no weight loss (Trustpilot, liv-pure.com). Small sample, but the pattern is consistent.
  • Not FDA-evaluated. Liv Pure is a dietary supplement. The FDA does not evaluate supplements for safety or effectiveness, so any ad that calls it "FDA approved" is wrong. We found no FDA warning letter or FTC action naming it.
  • No weight-loss evidence for the blend. Its liver-support ingredients (such as silymarin from milk thistle) have indirect evidence at best, and "dissolves fat overnight" is marketing, not science.
  • Refund route: orders go through ClickBank with a 60-day window; if the vendor doesn't respond, request the refund via clkbank.com.
  • Name confusion: "Livpure" is also an unrelated Indian water-purifier brand, so check you're reading about the supplement.
💊 Not medical advice. Talk to your doctor before taking any liver or weight-loss supplement, especially if you have liver disease or take prescription medication (milk thistle and similar ingredients can interact with medicines processed by the liver).
