What Fiverr Mira is

Fiverr's help center describes Mira as "Fiverr's AI headhunter". Its own product page at mira.fiverr.com puts it in one line: "Describe the role or job you're looking for, and let Mira find the right talent."

In practice, Mira does four jobs for you:

  1. Interviews you. It asks follow-up questions until your project is clear.
  2. Writes the job description. It fills in goals, budget, timeline and preferences.
  3. Reaches out to freelancers. It sends your job to eligible talent and asks them questions to check fit, availability and pricing.
  4. Hands you a shortlist. You get talent cards with a short reason why each person fits.

Mira also uses context from your Fiverr account, such as your order history, name, currency and time zone. Your Mira job descriptions live inside Mira, not on the older Brief management page.

Do you have access yet?

Maybe not. Fiverr says Mira "is currently rolling out gradually, so not all users have access yet."

  • Where it works: desktop browser and mobile browser.
  • Where it doesn't (yet): the Fiverr iOS and Android apps.
  • How to find it: look for Post a job description on the Fiverr home screen or main menu, or open mira.fiverr.com directly.

If you don't see it, you can still hire the classic way by searching gigs. Fiverr says access will come to more users over time.

How to use Fiverr Mira, step by step

Step 1: Start a conversation

Click Post a job description and tell Mira what you need. Write the way you would talk to a friend: "I need someone to fix my slow WordPress site before a product launch."

You can chat in your own language. But note that the final job description is always generated in English.

Step 2: Answer the follow-up questions

Mira keeps asking until all required fields are complete. A progress bar shows what's left. The required fields include:

  • Understanding the need
  • Business name
  • Project goals
  • Budget
  • Timeline
  • Search preferences (time zone, location, availability, experience or language)

Other fields depend on the type of service. Mira may also suggest optional fields. Fill them in; they improve your matches.

Step 3: Review and edit the job description

Once the required fields are done, you can review everything. Edit it by hand, or just ask Mira in the chat ("make the deadline 10 days, not 14"). You can also click Export to download it as a PDF or open it in HTML. That's handy if you want to keep a copy or share it with a partner.

Important note: read this draft slowly. Once you send your job description, you cannot edit or pause it. If you need a change later, you'll have to start a new project.

Step 4: Choose how fast you want results

When the job description is ready, you choose what happens next:

OptionWhat happensBest for
See my shortlistYou instantly get a list of matching freelancers to review and contact yourself.When you want to start messaging right now
Let Mira start (2, 4 or 12 hours)Mira contacts freelancers, confirms interest, availability, pricing and fit, then gives you up to three recommendations. You get an email when it's ready.When you can wait a few hours for better-vetted picks

Fiverr says a longer window gives Mira more time to search and "may result in stronger matches." If your deadline allows it, pick 12 hours.

Step 5: Compare your shortlist

Your job description page shows live updates as freelancers respond. When the shortlist arrives, you can:

  • Read why each freelancer is a good fit.
  • Click Compare talent to see everyone side by side.
  • Click Message to start a chat. The freelancer's inbox shows a summary and the full job description as a PDF.

Step 6: Hire, or search yourself

Not happy with the picks? Click Search Fiverr instead. It opens normal Fiverr search results already filtered by your job description. Nothing is lost.

Want to run another project at the same time? Click Start a new project. Fiverr says there's no limit on how many job descriptions you can send at once.

Step 7: Manage or delete your projects

Click your profile image, then Your projects, to switch between projects or delete one. Under Your profile, you can also fill in your business, brand voice and past projects so future job descriptions are better. To wipe everything, go to Privacy & data and click Delete your conversations. That permanently deletes your Mira history, but not your Fiverr account.

The under-$500 rule (read this before you set a budget)

This is the detail most people miss. Fiverr's help article says:

"If your budget is under $500, Mira doesn't send your job description to talent. Instead, you receive a filtered list of talent based on your job description details."

So the full headhunter service, where Mira contacts freelancers, confirms price and availability, and picks up to three, is for budgets of $500 and up. Below that, Mira works more like a smart search filter.

What that means for you

  • Small jobs ($50–$300): you still get a useful, filtered list. You just do the outreach yourself.
  • Mid-size jobs close to $500: if your real budget is, say, $450–$550, be honest. Don't inflate it to unlock outreach. Freelancers will quote to your number, and you'll pay it.
  • Bigger projects ($500+): this is where Mira saves the most time.

A brief template you can copy

Mira asks questions anyway, but starting with a clear brief means fewer rounds and better matches. Copy this, fill in the brackets, and paste it as your first message.

I need: [service, e.g. a WordPress speed fix / a 60-second product video / a logo refresh] About my business: [business name], [what you sell], [who your customers are] Goal: [the result you want, e.g. "mobile PageSpeed score above 80" or "a video for Instagram ads"] Scope - must have: - [deliverable 1] - [deliverable 2] - [file formats / sizes / number of revisions] Out of scope: [what you do NOT need] Budget: [$ amount, one number or a narrow range] Timeline: [deadline date + any fixed launch date] Preferences: [time zone overlap, language, experience level, industry experience] Examples I like: [1-3 links] - and why Examples I don't like: [1 link] - and why I will provide: [logins, brand files, copy, footage] How I'll judge success: [simple test you can check on delivery]

Why this works: it answers every required field Mira asks for, it tells freelancers what "done" means, and the "out of scope" line prevents surprise quotes later.

What hiring through Mira really costs

Fiverr's help article on Mira doesn't list any separate charge for using Mira. The cost shows up when you place an order with a freelancer. Three money tips

  1. Watch the $200 line. A $199 order costs $213.45 in total, while a $200 order costs $211.00. If a quote lands just under $200 and a small extra is useful, add it in the same payment.
  2. Bundle extras. Every payment carries its own fee. A $10 extra bought later costs $4.05 in fees on its own. Ask for one custom offer that covers everything.
  3. Tips have fees too. Fiverr's payments page says tips are separate payments with their own service fee. Tip once, not twice.

Tax may be added at checkout depending on your country. Fiverr always shows the final total before you confirm.

Tips and mistakes to avoid

Do

  • Fill in your Mira profile (business, brand voice, past projects). Mira uses it to personalize future job descriptions.
  • Give one clear success test, like "site loads under 2.5 seconds on mobile". Freelancers quote better when the target is measurable.
  • Pick the 12-hour window when your deadline allows it.
  • Export the PDF of your job description before sending, so you have a record.

Don't

  • Don't rush the review step. You can't edit after sending.
  • Don't expect to copy a job description. Fiverr says each one is built for a single project and can't be duplicated.
  • Don't assume teammates can see it. On team accounts, each member only sees the job descriptions they created.
  • Don't take the conversation off Fiverr. Keep messages, files and payments on the platform so buyer protection applies.
Good to know: freelancers are asked to answer Mira's first message within 24 hours. So if you choose "Let Mira start", a quiet first hour is normal.

Verdict

Fiverr Mira is a genuinely useful shortcut for custom projects of $500 or more. It writes a clean brief, does the outreach and hands you up to three vetted picks. Under $500 it's still handy as a smart filter, but you do the messaging. Prepare your brief before you start, check the draft carefully because it locks once sent, and budget for the 5.5% fee (plus $3.50 under $200).

FAQ

What is Fiverr Mira?

Mira is Fiverr's AI headhunter. You describe what you need, it builds a job description, contacts suitable freelancers and returns a curated shortlist of up to three recommendations.

Is Fiverr Mira free to use?

Fiverr's help article doesn't list a separate charge for Mira. You pay when you order: the standard buyer fee is 5.5% of the order plus $3.50 on orders under $200.

Why can't I see Mira on my account?

Mira is rolling out gradually, so not every user has it yet. It works on desktop and mobile web, but not in the iOS or Android apps.

What happens if my budget is under $500?

Mira doesn't send your job description to freelancers. Instead, you get a filtered list of talent based on your job details, and you contact them yourself.

Can I edit a Mira job description after sending it?

No. Once sent, you can't edit or pause it. You can delete the project or start a new one.

How long does Mira take to find freelancers?

You can see an instant list, or let Mira work for 2, 4 or 12 hours. A longer window gives it more time to find stronger matches.

Can I write to Mira in another language?

Yes. You can chat in your preferred language, but the job description is always generated in English.

Does Mira replace Fiverr search?

No. If you don't like the shortlist, click "Search Fiverr instead" to see normal search results filtered by your job description.
