$30 Essential: the PNG-only trap

The Essential package gives you three PNG files: your logo on its original background, on a transparent background, and a grayscale version on a transparent background. You also get full commercial rights.

That sounds fine until you read the small line on the pricing page: "Logo can not be edited after checkout." Fiverr also notes that PNG files can't be edited.

Why that matters

A PNG is a picture made of pixels. It looks sharp at the size it was exported, but it gets blurry when you blow it up. You can't change the font, the color or the spacing inside it. So with Essential:

  • A big banner, a shop sign or a vehicle wrap will likely look soft or pixelated.
  • If you spot a typo or want a darker shade next month, you can't fix it in the editor.
  • A printer or a designer who asks for "the vector file" will get nothing useful from you.

When $30 is enough

  • A hobby blog, a YouTube channel art test or a small Etsy shop that lives on screens only.
  • A placeholder logo for an MVP you plan to rebrand later.
  • A one-off event or internal project.
Important note: before you click Essential, finish every tweak in the editor first. Check the spelling of your business name, the colors and the layout on both a light and a dark background. After checkout, that version is final.

$60 Professional: the sweet spot

Professional is labeled "Popular" on Fiverr's page, and for once the label is fair. For $30 more you get everything a small business usually needs in the first year.

What the extra $30 buys

  • Three SVG vector files. Vectors scale to any size without losing quality. They are what printers, sign makers and web developers ask for.
  • Print-ready files for business cards, posters and billboards.
  • A favicon (256 x 256 px) for your website tab.
  • A brand style guide as a PDF, listing your colors, typography and visual rules. Hand it to anyone who designs for you later.
  • A social media kit with sized files for Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, YouTube, Pinterest and Etsy.
  • Zoom backgrounds with a repeating pattern of your logo icon.
  • One extra revision after checkout, so you can create one more downloadable variation.

Think about what those pieces would cost separately. Fiverr's own cost guide puts beginner logo design gigs at $5 to $75, and brand style guides and social kits are usually sold as extras on top. Getting the vectors, the guide and the kit together for $60 is good value for a tool-made logo.

If you will ever print the logo, put it on a sign, or hand it to another designer, treat $60 as the real price of Fiverr Logo Maker.

The one limit

You get exactly one revision after checkout. Use it wisely. A common smart move is to keep it in reserve for a seasonal color version or a stacked layout you discover you need later.

$90 Unlimited: who needs it

Unlimited includes every file from Professional. The difference is the editor: you can go back after checkout and create as many new logo variations as you like, and download them free. Fiverr's help center confirms that Unlimited buyers can create and download new variations of the same logo project at no extra cost, while other buyers pay for each new variation.

Unlimited makes sense if you:

  • Run seasonal campaigns and want holiday colors each quarter.
  • Expect to change your tagline, or add a location or sub-brand name.
  • Need many layouts: horizontal for the website, stacked for Instagram, icon-only for an app.
  • Are still testing your brand and want room to change your mind without paying again.

Skip it if you:

  • Already love the logo and just need the files.
  • Plan to hire a human designer within a year anyway.

One small catch: the editor works on desktop only. On mobile you can browse and pick designs, but you can't change colors, fonts or layout. So "unlimited" really means unlimited at your computer.

Rights, trademarks and refunds: read this before you pay

This section matters more than the price difference.

QuestionAnswer (all three packages)
Can I use it to make money?Yes. Full commercial rights: merchandise, web ads, books and more.
Is the license exclusive?No. You get a non-exclusive license.
Can I trademark it?No. Fiverr's help center says you can't resell, trademark or claim exclusive ownership of the design.
Can someone else get a similar logo?Possibly. Fiverr notes a design "could be sold multiple times", so customize it.
Can I cancel after buying?No. Logo Maker orders aren't eligible for cancellation, though revisions are possible on packages that include them.

Fiverr's Terms of Service describe the same thing in legal language: a worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive, irrevocable license to use the logo design with your brand name.

Important note: if a registered trademark is part of your business plan (for example, you plan to sell a product line nationally or raise money), no package of Fiverr Logo Maker solves that. Hire a designer who transfers full ownership, and talk to a trademark professional in your country.

What a cheap designer really costs

Fiverr's buyer service fee is 5.5% of the order, plus $3.50 on any order under $200. GigBuyerGuide checked well-reviewed logo gigs on 29 September 2026 and found a median starting price of $27.50. Add the fee and that becomes:

  • $27.50 × 5.5% = $1.51
  • + $3.50 small-order fee
  • = $32.51 before tax

So a basic designer gig and the $30 Logo Maker package cost about the same. But a starting package rarely includes vector source files or full ownership; those are often paid extras. And each extra you add later is a separate payment with its own fee.

My rule: if you need a logo this week and the business is small or new, Logo Maker Professional wins. If the logo is the face of a brand you will defend for years, hire a designer and make sure the gig includes source files and an ownership transfer.

Tips to get the most from any package

  1. Design on desktop. The editor doesn't work on mobile.
  2. Customize hard. Change the icon color, font and spacing so your logo drifts away from the base template.
  3. Test on dark and light backgrounds before you pay.
  4. Download the sample kit from the pricing page to see the file types before you buy.
  5. Save your files in two places (cloud and local) on the day you buy.

Verdict

For most people, the $60 Professional package is the one to buy. The $30 Essential tier is a fair deal only if your logo will never leave a screen and you're sure about the design today. The $90 Unlimited tier is a convenience upgrade, not a quality upgrade. And if you need a trademark, skip logo makers altogether and hire a designer.

FAQ

How much does Fiverr Logo Maker cost in 2026?

There are three one-time packages: Essential $30, Professional $60 and Unlimited $90. Designing and customizing is free; you pay only to download.

Can I edit my logo after buying the $30 package?

No. Fiverr's pricing page says the Essential logo can't be edited after checkout, and it comes as PNG files only.

Which Fiverr Logo Maker package includes SVG files?

Professional ($60) and Unlimited ($90). Both include three SVG files plus print-ready files and a favicon.

Can I trademark a Fiverr Logo Maker logo?

No. You receive a non-exclusive license with full commercial rights, but Fiverr's help center says you can't trademark, resell or claim exclusive ownership of the design.

Can I get a refund on Fiverr Logo Maker?

Fiverr's help center says Logo Maker orders aren't eligible for cancellation after purchase. You can still make revisions if your package includes them.

Is the $90 Unlimited package worth it?

Only if you'll keep creating new versions, such as seasonal colors or different layouts. Otherwise, the $60 package covers the same files.

Is Fiverr Logo Maker cheaper than hiring a designer?

Usually, yes, and it's much faster. A basic Fiverr logo gig at the $27.50 median comes to about $32.51 after the buyer fee, but it rarely includes vectors or full rights at that price.
