How the TrustScore works

Every product on this site is scored 1–10 as a weighted average of four sub-scores:

Sub-scoreWeightWhat it measures
Quality & depth35%How substantive the product is versus its promises — depth of the reading, program or report.
Value for money30%What you get for the price, including cheaper alternatives that exist.
Delivery & UX20%Speed, reliability and polish of the purchase-to-product experience.
Support & guarantee15%Refund policy reality, support responsiveness, company track record.

Our editorial rules

1. We buy with our own money. Orders are placed like a normal customer's, through the public checkout.

2. The cons are mandatory. No review publishes without at least three genuine drawbacks. If we can't find three, we haven't looked hard enough.

3. Scores are opinions, dated. Every review carries its update date. Products change; so do scores.

4. Belief-based products get honest framing. Psychic readings, numerology and manifestation are not science, and we never pretend otherwise. We score the product execution — delivery, depth, fairness — not whether metaphysics is true.

5. Commissions don't move scores. We earn affiliate commissions on most products reviewed, including products we score 7.2. The ranking is ours; the disclosure is on every page. Full disclosure.

6. Supplements are scored as products, not medicine. We evaluate formulation transparency, vendor track record, refund reality and independent customer sentiment — and we never imply treatment of any condition. Products making claims they cannot support lose points for it.
